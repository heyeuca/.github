## Who we are

euca started in 2025. We make software for people who'd rather open something
and find it already sorted out than spend an evening setting it up. A few apps
later, the way we work hasn't changed much: figure out what a product is really
for, leave out the rest, and hold that shape as it grows.

We're not trying to grow the catalog. A few things we can look after properly
feels better than a long list we can't.

## What we make

Small, native apps for Apple platforms.

- **Ready** — very little to set up
- **Focused** — new work goes deeper, not wider
- **Quiet** — content to stay out of the way

## What we leave out

- Options where a decision would do
- Features that try to cover every preference
- Updates without a reason

> If you need the broader tool, these are intentionally the wrong ones.

## Contributing

[Tilde](https://github.com/heyeuca/Tilde) is MIT licensed, and issues and pull
requests are welcome.

- **Bugs and rough edges** — always good to hear about
- **Fixes** — a pull request is the easiest way
- **Ideas** — still early days, so send them; the ones that fit what the app is
  for are the ones that get built
